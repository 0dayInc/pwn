# T0828: Loss of Productivity and Revenue

- Catalog: [T0828](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T0828)
- Kind: technique · ATT&CK 19.2
- Domains: ics-attack
- Version: 1.0

## Technique

Adversaries may cause loss of productivity and revenue through disruption and even damage to the availability and integrity of control system operations, devices, and related processes. This technique may manifest as a direct effect of an ICS-targeting attack or tangentially, due to an IT-targeting attack against non-segregated environments. 

In cases where these operations or services are brought to a halt, the loss of productivity may eventually present an impact for the end-users or consumers of products and services. The disrupted supply-chain may result in supply shortages and increased prices, among other consequences. 

A ransomware attack on an Australian beverage company resulted in the shutdown of some manufacturing sites, including precautionary halts to protect key systems. (Citation: Paganini, Pierluigi June 2020) The company announced the potential for temporary shortages of their products following the attack. (Citation: Paganini, Pierluigi June 2020) (Citation: Lion Corporation June 2020) 

In the 2021 Colonial Pipeline ransomware incident, the pipeline was unable to transport approximately 2.5 million barrels of fuel per day to the East Coast.  (Citation: Colonial Pipeline Company May 2021)

## Procedure

1. **Applicability gate.** If none of the platforms/domains exist in scope, record `T0828 N/A: <reason>` and stop.
2. **Instance inventory.** Hosts, identities, SaaS apps, ICS nodes, mobile devices. One workstation is not an inventory.
3. **Oracle.** Define pass/fail: procedure succeeded (control bypassed / action completed) vs blocked vs detected.
4. **Execute / emulate.** Follow the technique description as the minimum procedure set. Persist command output, proxy logs, or pcap under `/tmp` or the engagement dir and **re-read** it.
5. **Sub-techniques.** If this ID has children, open each child file and run it. Parent-only testing is incomplete when sub-techniques exist.
6. **Detection check.** Using the same evidence, ask whether the listed data sources would have fired. Missing telemetry is a finding (visibility gap), not a skip.
7. **Mitigation negation.** For each mitigation below, retry with the control on and bypassed. Unchanged oracle = control failed.
8. **CAPEC / CWE close-out.** Run each mapped CAPEC file in the `capec` skill, then related CWE files in `cwe`. ATT&CK without CWE is a procedure note, not a weakness finding.
9. **Report.** Cite `T0828`, tactic, CAPEC/CWE IDs, instance, oracle, evidence path.
